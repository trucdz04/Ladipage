## 08 — FAQ (accordion)
1. **What is an AI video generator?** — An AI video generator uses machine learning to create videos from text prompts or images. You provide a description or upload an image, and the AI produces a short video clip with motion, animation, and effects applied automatically.
2. **How long does it take to generate a video?** — Most videos are generated within 30-60 seconds depending on the complexity and resolution. Higher resolution or longer clips may take a bit more time. You will be notified as soon as your video is ready.
3. **What kinds of videos can I create?** — A wide variety — animated scenes, product showcases, social media clips, artistic visualizations, talking head animations, and more. Supports different styles and motion types.
4. **How do I get the best results?** — Write detailed, specific prompts describing the motion, style, and scene. For image-to-video, use clear high-quality images. The more context you give the AI, the closer the output matches your vision.
5. **What resolutions and formats are supported?** — Multiple resolutions up to 720p HD. Download in standard formats like MP4. Lower resolutions process faster; higher provide better quality.
6. **Can I use the generated videos commercially?** — Yes, videos are yours to use for personal or commercial purposes including social media, ads, websites, presentations. Check terms of service.
